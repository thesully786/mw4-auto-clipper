Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MW4 Auto Clipper handles information when users connect the application to supported third-party platforms.

Information We Process

The application may process information required to authenticate with supported platforms and perform user-authorized actions, such as basic account information and authorization credentials.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide requested application features, including authentication and user-authorized uploading or publishing of content.

Third-Party Services

MW4 Auto Clipper may connect to third-party services such as TikTok through their authorized APIs. Information handled by those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

Data Sharing

MW4 Auto Clipper does not sell personal information. Information is shared with third-party platforms only when necessary to perform actions authorized by the user.

User Control

Users control whether they authorize the application to access a supported platform and may revoke platform access through the relevant platform's account settings.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application or its integrations change.
